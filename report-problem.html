SUPPORT

Report a Problem

If you experience a problem with your Learnpidia account, rewards, digital products or another app feature, please use the support channel available inside the Learnpidia app.

When reporting an issue, include:

• A short description of what happened.

• The Learnpidia feature involved.

• What you expected to happen.

• What actually happened.

• Any relevant error message or screenshot.

Do not send your password or other sensitive account credentials.
