HELP CENTER

Need help with Learnpidia?

Use this page for general guidance about your Learnpidia account, Learnpidia Coins, reward activities and digital products.

Account & Sign-In

Make sure you have an active Learnpidia account and a working internet connection. If you cannot sign in or create an account, use the support channel available inside the app.

Learnpidia Coins

Learnpidia Coins are the in-app reward balance used for eligible Learnpidia features and digital products. Reward availability can depend on the applicable activity and daily limits.

Digital Products

Eligible digital products can be unlocked using the required number of Learnpidia Coins. Product access is subject to your account status and the applicable product rules.

Still Having a Problem?

If the issue is not covered here, use the Report a Problem page or the support channel inside the Learnpidia app.
